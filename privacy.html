Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Rallysis is developed by Hanqing Li. This policy explains what Rallysis does with your information.

1. Data we collect

We do not collect personal data. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no tracking. We do not receive your videos, photos, device identifiers, or usage history.

2. Videos and photos

Videos you choose or record are stored in the app's private storage so that you can reopen and edit them. You can delete videos and history at any time from Settings.

3. Weather in the watermark

When you export a clip with the watermark, the app may read the video's capture date and location metadata and send that date and approximate coordinates to Open-Meteo to look up the weather at that time and place. No video, photo, or identifier is sent. If the lookup fails, the watermark shows only the information available in the video.

4. Camera and microphone

Camera and microphone access is used only while you record a match inside the app.

5. Purchases

The optional lifetime unlock is processed by Apple's App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple may provide us with anonymous transaction status so that the app can unlock the purchase and restore it.

6. Network

Network access is used for App Store product information, purchases, and restore purchases, and for the optional weather lookup described above.

7. Children

Rallysis does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes

We may add features that change how data is handled. If that happens, we will update this page and the date above before those features ship.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub

Rallysis 由 Hanqing Li 开发。本页说明羽律/Rallysis 如何处理你的信息。

  • 儿童：不会有意收集任何人的信息，包括儿童。
