Frequently asked questions

Where are my videos stored?

Videos you import or record are copied into the app's private storage so the app can reopen them. You can remove them from Settings.

How do I delete my data?

Open Me → Settings and choose Clear all history. This deletes history records and the video copies created by the app.

Why did the app ask for camera or photo access?

Camera and microphone access is used for recording a match. Photo access is used to choose an existing badminton video.

Can I remove the watermark?

The free version adds a small Rallysis watermark to exports. A one-time lifetime unlock lets you turn it off from the export options in the studio or in Settings. There is no subscription.

Why does the watermark show a place and weather?

If the video includes capture time and location metadata, the watermark can show the date and place. When weather lookup is enabled, it also shows the local weather at export time. If a lookup is unavailable, the watermark shows only what the video already contains.

I bought the lifetime unlock. How do I restore it?

Open the unlock screen and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID used for the purchase.

Rally detection is not finding my rallies.

Landscape footage with the full court visible works best. Very short clips, heavy motion blur, or videos where the court is mostly out of frame can reduce accuracy.

支持（简体中文）

支持：GitHub Issues

  • 视频在哪？ 导入或录制的视频会复制到 App 私有存储，方便再次打开。可在设置中删除。
  • 如何删除数据？ 「我的 → 设置 → 清空全部记录」会删除记录和视频副本。
  • 相机/相册权限？ 相机与麦克风用于录制比赛，相册用于选择已有的羽毛球视频。
  • 可以去除水印吗？ 免费版导出会带一个小号羽律水印。一次永久解锁后，可在剪辑页或设置的导出选项中关闭。没有订阅。
  • 水印里的地点和天气是怎么来的？ 如果视频包含拍摄时间和位置信息，水印会显示日期和地点；启用天气查询时还会显示当地天气。查询不可用时就只显示视频已有的信息。
  • 如何恢复购买？ 在解锁页点「恢复购买」，并使用购买时的同一 Apple ID。
  • 识别不到回合？ 横屏、完整球场的画面效果最好；过短或严重模糊的视频会影响识别。
